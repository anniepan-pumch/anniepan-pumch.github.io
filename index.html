About

I am a PhD Candidate in Neurology at Peking Union Medical College Hospital in Beijing, China.

My research interests include neuroimaging and cerebral small vessel disease, with a particular interest in quantitative susceptibility mapping (QSM), diffusion tensor imaging (DTI), functional MRI (fMRI), and medical artificial intelligence.

This website brings together my research interests and public code repositories for brain MRI analysis.

Research interests

NEUROVASCULAR IMAGING

Cerebral small vessel disease

MRI markers of cerebrovascular disease and the study of brain structure.

QUANTITATIVE MRI

Susceptibility mapping

QSM and quantitative approaches to characterizing brain tissue.

STRUCTURE & FUNCTION

Diffusion & functional MRI

DTI and fMRI methods for studying white matter and brain function.

COMPUTATIONAL METHODS

Medical AI & deep learning

Computational approaches to neuroimaging analysis and neurological research.
